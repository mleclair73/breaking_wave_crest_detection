# Argus Rectified Videos (DUNEX FRF) — Stage-1 input

36 rectified Argus optical videos (`ArgusFF_YYYYMMDDThhmmssZ_RectifiedVideo.avi`, ~40G) from the
Argus tower at the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers **Field Research Facility (FRF)**, Duck, NC,
during the **DUNEX** campaign (Sep–Oct 2021). This is the primary input to Stage-1 breaking-wave
crest segmentation.

## Source of record
Dryad — **DOI [10.5061/dryad.r2280gbsn](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.r2280gbsn)**,
*"Detections of breaking wave crests in surfzone optical video."* Re-download from Dryad to
reproduce.

## Origin and processing

The local analysis copy was re-encoded to reduce disk usage. The original files
and processing utilities are not part of this source distribution.
